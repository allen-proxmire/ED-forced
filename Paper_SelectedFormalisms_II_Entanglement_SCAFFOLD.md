## Abstract (draft — honest)

Event Density (ED) reads the *commitment* of events, the arrow of time made physical, as the act that individuates one outcome from many. On that reading, entanglement is the *pre-commitment* regime (unresolved individuation, not action-at-a-distance) and measurement is the commitment act itself. This paper works the quantum-foundations sector, and is candid about its tiers, which are lower than the gravitational sector's. It reproduces the bipartite entanglement formalism — tensor product, Schmidt decomposition, monogamy, no-signaling, von Neumann entropy — and the Bell–Tsirelson structure. These are *reproduced, not re-derived*: they are form-forced conditional on a stack of V5 postulates, the Tsirelson bound is inherited, and the irreducibility that defines entanglement is an assertion rather than a theorem; ED adds no new bound. Two results are genuinely strong: no-signaling is over-determined (three independent locks), and ED delivers locality without superdeterminism, because the controlled inter-region channel capacity is exactly zero (a measured result) and the arrow is forward. Measurement is the commitment act, the same move that orients time, with the pointer basis einselected (a reconstruction carrying a Solèr residual) and no observer required. The collapse variable is time, not energy — a distinctive but unconfirmed structural synthesis, with the rate `τ ∝ ℏ/E_G` reproduced but `E_G` identified, not computed. The sector's one forward bet is a sharp, architecture-independent ceiling on macroscopic superposition; its distinctive content is the wall's *shape*, while the mass scale is a two-point extrapolation now pressured from below by matter-wave interference at ≥170 kDa. We state plainly what the sector does not deliver.
