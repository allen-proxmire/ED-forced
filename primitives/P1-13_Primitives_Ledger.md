### Note on the two-tier directory
The `primitives/` folder also holds `concepts/` — **process-level ontological treatments** written *before* the canonical P01–P13 consolidation. **Cross-reference hazard (from the README):** the `concepts/` files carry their **own independent 01–13 numbering that does NOT match the canonical P-numbers** (e.g. concept "Primitive 05" = Event Density, not P05 polarity-transport). Always resolve a primitive citation by **P-number via the top-level `Pnn_*.md` cards**, never by a concept file's internal number. Not tiered here — they are definitional depth beneath the axioms, not additional axioms.

### Honest state
These are **postulates, not results.** 087 is explicit: no claim the 13 are forced from a deeper layer, no claim of minimality (the set is *necessary-for-current-derivations* — removing any one blocks a downstream derivation — not minimal in an absolute sense), no claim ED is the only consistent substrate ontology, no derivation from nothing. The open questions about the set itself (minimality; whether any primitive derives another; P06's derivability; the arrow's P11+P13+V1 redundancy; the P14 placeholder) are carried in the `foundations/` ledger. **Defensible headline:** *thirteen postulated axioms — the corpus's single "conditional on," honestly held as posited (not minimal, not forced from below), with the whole tiered-claims sweep standing downstream of them.*
