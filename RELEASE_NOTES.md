# Event Density — a substrate ontology, and its audit trail

**One primitive does the work: the arrow of time — the irreversible act of commitment.**

Most attempts at unification add structure. This one removes it. Event Density starts from a discrete relational substrate with no intrinsic metric, no background coordinates, and no dimensionless constants of its own, and asks what a single irreversible act can be made to carry.

The answer, so far, is more than expected — and the claim the program actually rests on is this:

> **The thing that selects a measurement outcome in quantum mechanics and the thing that supplies a preferred time in gravity are not two things. They are one primitive.** The Problem of Time does not get bridged. It stops arising.

## What is here

**242 papers, 506 documents, 16 arc ledgers.** Weak-field gravity reproduced, MOND emerging natively as a horizon interference term rather than a modification, quantum mechanics reconstructed from relational lattices, gauge structure from indistinguishable channels, charge quantization from winding, rest mass from binding rather than a field.

**And a complete record of everything that failed.**

That second part is not modesty. It is the point.

## What makes this repository unusual

Every load-bearing claim carries a **tier**: derived, grounded, inherited, postulated, measured, open. Not as decoration — as a contract. A claim tiered `Grounded` names the postulate it rests on. A claim tiered `Inherited` names where the number came from. A claim tiered `Open` says what would close it.

**The ledgers record corrections against the theory as carefully as results for it.** A flagship prediction that went from *confirmed* to *untested* in one day when a systematics critique checked out. A repair carried for two days that turned out to invert the problem rather than fix it. A summary that overstated the arc's own open questions by two. Those are in here, dated, with the reasoning.

Six automated checks run over the corpus and fail on drift: stale claims, broken provenance, contradictory definitions across documents, unverified rows, dead pointers. **Each was built after a defect got through.**

## What this is not

Not peer-reviewed. Not complete. **No distinctive prediction is confirmed.** Two structural questions are open — why gauge multiplicities are `{1,2,3}`, and whether exact charge conservation forces anomaly freedom. One prerequisite is unproven: universal free fall. The dimensionless constants are inherited, by design and by argument.

**"Form-complete" here is a coverage claim** — the substrate fixes the *form* of a law while the universe supplies its numbers. It is not closure, not unique derivation, and not empirical validation. The report says so in its first paragraph.
