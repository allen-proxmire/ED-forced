## 1. The column the ledger left blank

The companion article, *How Our Best Theories Coarse-Grain Reality*, reads each celebrated symmetry of modern physics as the **receipt for a discard**, and its symmetry entries run on one mechanism: **order-erasure.** A coarse-graining is an average, and an average is permutation-blind — it does not remember which of its terms came first. So a law written in the blurred variables cannot contain an arrow, not because reality is reversible, but because the vocabulary has forgotten the word "which way." That single mechanism buys reversibility, unitarity, frame-democracy, and diffeomorphism-invariance, and the article's recipe table accounts for each.

But scan that table and something is missing. There is no **conservation law** in it — no conservation of energy, no conservation of momentum. This is not an oversight; it is a boundary. Conservation laws are the Noether charges of continuous **translation** symmetries, and translation symmetry is not erased order. It is something else: the assumption that the arena is **uniform**. The ledger built the mechanism for the order-symmetries and stopped. This article builds the second column.

## 3. Why conservation is a *different* discard

Put the two families of symmetry on separate axes.

- **Order-erasure** (the companion ledger): the blur forgets the *sequence* of events. What it buys — reversibility, unitarity, frame-democracy — are all statements about the **order** of what happens.
- **Backdrop-uniformity** (this column): the blur treats the arena as *the same everywhere and everywhen*. What it buys — the Noether conservation laws, energy from time-translation invariance and momentum from space-translation invariance — are statements about the **uniformity** of where and when it happens.

These are distinct axes, and the distinction is sharper than mere independence. General relativity's diffeomorphism invariance is a *gauge* symmetry and carries no Noether charge — which is precisely why GR has no global energy; a conservation law needs a *rigid, global* translation symmetry, and that is the thing a changing backdrop destroys. Order-erasure and backdrop-uniformity are read off different discard-columns. That is why conservation never appeared in the order-erasure table, and why it needs its own.

## 4. The conservation column

| Kept | Assumed / discarded | The artifact-symmetry it buys | Where it leaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| the smooth conserved density (energy, momentum) | the **exact uniformity of the backdrop** — perfect translation invariance | the **conservation law** | wherever the backdrop is *not* uniform — a **dynamic** spacetime — with the leak's size set by how much the backdrop changes across the region measured |

A conservation law is what you get by *assuming* a perfectly uniform arena. Where the assumption is exact, the law is exact. Where the arena actually changes, the conserved quantity **leaks** — and the leak is a **gradient, not a cliff**: negligible across a lab bench over an afternoon, total across the cosmos over its history.

## 5. The mechanism is already in the primitives *(structural)*

The column needs no new postulate. Its mechanism is latent in P13 and P11, and only wants connecting.

**P13 makes energy the Noether charge — where a stationary background exists.** The substrate law carries no explicit time-dependence ($\partial_t$ is a substrate symmetry), so wherever the background can be treated as stationary — a closed system, a lab, a static field — energy is the conserved Noether charge of time-translation, generated by the Hamiltonian (`Paper_006_5`), conserved exactly. What holds in *full* generality, even in a changing spacetime, is only the covariant identity $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$ — and that follows from general covariance (the Bianchi identity), **not** from time-translation symmetry, and it is **not** a conservation law that yields a global energy. A global conserved energy needs more: a timelike Killing symmetry — a genuinely stationary *state*.

**P11 removes that stationarity.** Commitment only accumulates; the substrate's actual history is never stationary, so there is no timelike Killing symmetry of what actually happens, and therefore **no globally conserved energy.** The split:

> **Where the background is stationary, energy is exactly conserved — P13's Noether charge. Where it is not — a dynamic spacetime — the global energy leaks, because the arrow (P11) leaves no stationary state for it to be the charge of.**

This is the companion ledger's signature — *"a discarded column never fully vanishes; it leaks"* — carried by conservation, with the substrate's one genuine irreversibility as the source.

**And the apparent conflict with the corpus dissolves.** `Paper_006_5` lists falsifier F4: *substrate-level non-conservation of energy from time-translation symmetry* — which would break the Noether identification of $\hat H$ as energy. F4 targets non-conservation in a **closed, stationary** setting, where the timelike symmetry holds; the leak occurs where that stationarity is **absent** (a dynamic backdrop), so Noether yields no global charge and non-conservation is expected, not a refutation. The Noether identification (F4-protected, closed systems) and the global leak (dynamic spacetimes) are the two halves of the same primitive pair — P13 the symmetric law, P11 the non-stationary state.

## 7. The leak has a cosmological profile: loud early, silent at the end *(interpretive, grounded)*

Because the leak tracks the backdrop's non-uniformity, it is not a fixed fact but a changing one, and the corpus supplies both ends of its history.

In the **dynamic, structured epoch** the backdrop changes fastest, and the leak is largest. The redshifting cosmic microwave background is this leak made visible: every relic photon has been losing energy for the age of the universe as space stretches, and that energy is simply not conserved. Standard general relativity says exactly this — there is no global energy in a non-stationary spacetime, so the photon energy is not conserved and "goes" nowhere. The substrate reading offers no new answer; it *names the source* of the bare GR fact — the expanding backdrop, the arrow — and files it in the same discard-ledger as reversibility. (The ED corpus had reproduced the expanding-universe equations without ever pausing on the point; the column is where it pauses.)

At the other end, the picture **changes character.** `Paper_ED_Cos_05` reads dark energy as the late-universe **saturation regime**, in which "expansion-driven dilution drives the substrate toward uniform-$\Psi$" — a de Sitter end-state. De Sitter never stops expanding, so a lone photon there would still redshift; what changes is that the redshiftable *content* dilutes to nothing, and the geometry becomes **self-similar and maximally symmetric** — it admits a timelike Killing symmetry (in the static patch), and with it a well-defined conserved energy returns. Not because the arena stops changing, but because it becomes stationary *in form* and empties of anything left to leak. Conservation is therefore not simply "broken": it is absent where the universe is busy and structured, and recovered where it has smoothed to a symmetric, near-empty end. The old intuition that the ultimate uniformity *returns* the symmetry — a cousin of Penrose's conformal endpoint — is, on this reading, grounded in a standing ED result rather than asserted.

## Honest close

The companion ledger showed that every famous symmetry is a receipt for something a blur threw away. Conservation is no exception — but it is the receipt for a *different* throwing-away: not the order of events, but the uniformity of the stage. Assume the stage never changes and you are handed a conservation law; let the stage change — let the universe expand, collapse, or ripple — and the law leaks, in exact proportion to the change. In Event Density the stage does change, because commitment only ever accumulates, and so the leak is not a flaw to be patched but the arrow of time, showing up one last place: in the books where physics counts what is supposed to be conserved. Local conservation stays perfect. Global conservation was always on loan from a stage that, in a becoming universe, was never quite still.

None of this is new physics; it is standard general relativity, read from underneath. What the column offers is not proof but shape — one more symmetry returned to its discard, and the two columns of ED's own ledger, at last, set side by side as a companion pair.
