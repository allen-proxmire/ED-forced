## Abstract

A quantum-statistical plasmonic metacrystal (LSU, 2026) sorts light not by wavelength or polarization but by its **quantum statistics** — the many-particle correlation structure — routing distinct statistical states down distinct paths and *preserving* them ("robust transport"), producing programmable "quantum statistical bands." The routed quantity is correlation structure, which is exactly the variable the V5 cross-chain kernel carries. We show the device is a **fifth face of the single bounded V5 envelope `W_max`** of `Paper_V5UnifiedBudget`, in the **broadcast-type live-redundancy family** with the Class-C plateau (058) and Quantum Darwinism (record-bandwidth). The mapping turns on one hinge, taken from `Paper_QuantumDarwinism_RecordBandwidth`: V5 correlation bifurcates on P11 commitment — **committed records cost no budget (unbounded); only live, uncommitted, coherent correlation is capped.** So the arc has a single load-bearing gate: *the routed statistical correlation must stay live* (coherent, uncommitted through the routing), evidenced by the device preserving nonclassical statistics rather than reading them out. Given the gate, the per-locus live cap (Darwinism §4.1, hub topology) applies directly: `N_bands^max ~ W_max/w̄_band`, sharp in correlation content and a band in the lab proxy (slit/mode count) by R1, and **commensurate** (O(1) ratio) with the Class-C plateau and the GHZ-width ceiling by R2 (extended: `1 : 1 : 0.88 : r_QD : r_route`). The discriminator from ordinary plasmonic loss is threefold: a *saturation* (not a monotonic decline), a *shared-budget crosstalk* (routing more bands degrades the preservation of all of them, because they compete for one `W_max`), and an *architecture-independent onset in content*. The absolute scale stays inherited; the shape, the crosstalk, and the commensurability are the falsifiable content.

## 2. The gate: live vs committed (make-or-break)

`Paper_QuantumDarwinism_RecordBandwidth` §2–§3 proves the hinge: V5 correlation splits on P11 commitment. **Committed records cost no V5 budget** (they are recorded structure, persist channel-free per A1, cannot be erased to refund — hence everyday classical redundancy is unbounded). **Only live, uncommitted, coherent correlation is what `W_max` caps** (monogamy pair-tangles, Class-C encoding coherence, Page straddling entanglement, GHZ branch structure — all live).

So the entire arc turns on one question about the metacrystal:

> **Is the routed statistical correlation live (coherent, uncommitted) or committed (detected/recorded)?**

- **If committed** — the device reads out the statistics and forwards a classical record — then the correlation is *free*, there is *no* V5 ceiling, and the arc ends here (the routing limit is then purely plasmonic/engineering).
- **If live** — the device performs *coherent* routing that *preserves* the nonclassical statistics without collapsing them — then the correlation is *budgeted*, and §§3–5 follow.

The device's stated behavior ("robust transport" that maintains the defining statistical properties, sorting by *quantum* statistical distinctions rather than by a classical readout) is evidence for the **live** branch: coherence preserved, not committed. **But this is the load-bearing step and it is not established here** — it must be shown, for the specific platform, that the routed correlation remains uncommitted through the metacrystal (e.g. preserved nonclassicality / Wigner negativity of a routed state, not a which-band classical tag). Falsifier F-LIVE (§6).

## 3. Given the gate: the per-locus live cap applies

Routing many statistical bands through a **shared** structure (common meta-atoms) is **hub topology** — many correlations simultaneously live at a shared routing locus — which is exactly the configuration Darwinism §4.1 caps (and the configuration where the per-locus cap *does* bite; contrast path topology, §4). By P-V5-Budget + P04 additivity, the shared locus's live weight partitions across the routed bands:

$$
N_{\text{bands}}^{\max}\;\lesssim\;\frac{W_{\max}}{\bar w_{\text{band}}},
$$

with `w̄_band` the mean live V5 weight per routed statistical band. This is the same integral `∫K_V5 = W_max` as monogamy and Class-C, spent on routed statistical bands instead of entanglement partners or encoding channels. It is therefore the **broadcast-type, ρ_loc = 1 family** (`Paper_V5UnifiedBudget` §5): one budget, a new way of spending it.

## 4. Topology caveat (stated, load-bearing)

Per Darwinism §4.3, the per-locus cap constrains **hub** topology (many bands live at a shared locus) — the natural reading of a single metacrystal all light passes through. It does **not**, by itself, cap a **path**-topology router in which each band traverses independent loci carrying O(1) links each. If the metacrystal is effectively path-like, converting the per-locus cap into a ceiling on the *number of simultaneously-preserved bands* requires **P-QD-LiveWeight** (the declared postulate of `Paper_QuantumDarwinism_RecordBandwidth` §5: sustained live correlation, including branch-basis, consumes pairwise V5 weight). Determining the metacrystal's effective topology (hub vs path) is a required input; it sets whether the ceiling is derived (hub) or postulate-conditional (path).

## 5. R1, R2, and the discriminator from plasmonic loss

Given §§2–3 (live + hub), the two forced relations of `Paper_V5UnifiedBudget` transfer with no extra assumption:

- **R1 — sharp in content, band in the proxy.** The ceiling sits at fixed live correlation content per locus for every metacrystal design; in the lab proxy (number of slits / modes / nominal bands `N`) it is a *band*, because the proxy→content conversion `α_route` (bands→per-locus live content) varies by design. Same figure as the Class-A wall and Class-C plateau: sharp in internal content, a band in the engineering proxy. `α_route` is architecture-specific and inherited/measured (evaluate under `Paper_ProxyConversionDoctrine`).
- **R2 (extended) — commensurate ceilings.** Being the same `W_max` spent on the same category (live broadcast-type redundancy), the routing ceiling is commensurate — O(1) ratio `r_route` — with the Class-C plateau and the GHZ-width ceiling: `W_V5 : B_cross,max : B_ent,max : r_QD : r_route = 1 : 1 : 0.88 : r_QD : r_route`. Observing the routing ceiling would pin, and be pinned by, the other faces.

**Discriminator from ordinary plasmonic loss** (the point of the exercise). Ohmic/plasmonic loss and a V5 budget ceiling are separable by three signatures:

1. **Saturation, not decline.** Plasmonic loss attenuates each channel smoothly and monotonically with propagation/interaction; the V5 budget produces a *plateau/ceiling* in the number (or joint fidelity) of simultaneously-preserved statistical bands.
2. **Shared-budget crosstalk (the fingerprint).** Loss degrades each routed state *independently*; the budget makes them *compete* for one `W_max`, so adding bands degrades the preservation of **all** of them. That coupling has no counterpart in independent-channel loss.
3. **Architecture-independent onset in content + commensurability.** By R1 the onset is at fixed content regardless of design (a band in the proxy); by R2 it is commensurate with the Class-C/GHZ ceilings. No noise or loss model reproduces a content-fixed, cross-arena-commensurate saturation.

## 7. Falsification Criteria

- **F-LIVE (the gate):** demonstration that the metacrystal's routing *commits/records* the statistical state (a classical which-band tag, nonclassicality not preserved through routing) — the correlation is then free, and ED predicts *no* V5 ceiling for this device (routing limited only by plasmonic loss). This is the arc's make-or-break.
- **F-SAT:** the number / joint fidelity of simultaneously-preserved statistical bands continues to rise with design scale-up with no saturation, once plasmonic loss is controlled for — no finite envelope in this arena (parallels Paper_058 F-form).
- **F-CROSSTALK:** routed bands degrade *independently* (no shared-budget coupling — adding bands does not degrade the others beyond independent loss) — falsifies the single-`W_max` reading here.
- **F-R2:** a routing ceiling inferred at content inconsistent (after the common `ℓ_V5` renormalization) with the Class-C plateau / GHZ ceiling — falsifies the fifth-face consolidation.
