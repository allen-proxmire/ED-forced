# `N_bw` has no consumer — and the bandwidth gap with a primitive's profile is the other bound

**2026-09-07.** The consumer check that closes the evening's P14 thread. Run in a read-only session, re-run and verified here.

---

## 1. The check: does anything actually use `N_bw`?

**No.** Every remaining mention of `N_bw` in `physics-papers/` is either **one of today's own flags** or a passing quotation of the `min` structure. `Paper_056` — the paper whose wall Class A is supposed to bind — **never uses it**. `Paper_055` uses it only for the taxonomy row **whose provenance was already found empty** (it cites `Paper_056` for a claim `Paper_056` does not make).

> **`N_bw` has no numerical consumer and no derivational consumer anywhere in the corpus. Its entire live role is one label in a taxonomy that was already known to be broken.**

**So the definitional gap under `N_bw` is real, and nothing depends on it.** It gets a one-line note, not a constitutional decision.

---

## 2. The sharpening that inverts the conclusion

An earlier framing had it that the singularity family needs an **upper** bound (per-cell capacity) while `N_bw` needs a **lower** one (a floor), so P14 would be bundling two commitments. **True, but they are not disjoint:**

$$N_{\mathrm{bw}} = B_{\max} / b_{\min}$$

needs **both**. The singularity family's per-cell capacity **is `N_bw`'s upper half.** So the accounting is:

| bound | needed by | status |
|---|---|---|
| **upper — per-cell capacity** | the singularity family **and** `N_bw` | **already named: `P-Locus-Bandwidth-Bound`** |
| **lower — the floor** | `N_bw` only | **named nowhere** |

**One of the two is already in the corpus.** But that is not the interesting part.

## 3. Apply the profile test to each, and the answer flips

The test proposed for whether something deserves primitive status: *does it earn its keep across arcs the way the thirteen do?*

- **The floor:** one arc, one object, **zero consumers.**
- **The capacity:** `P-Locus-Bandwidth-Bound` is declared across **four folders** — `foundations`, `gravity`, `q-compute`, `soft-matter` — *(measured here; a parallel count including `substrate-evaluation` and `black-hole` gives five)* — and reaches in-process experimental protocols under `predictions/`. **By Target #20's own ladder that is the `≥4`-paper cross-cutting class, the tier the doc calls the framework's standing commitments.**

> **The bandwidth bound with a primitive's profile is the CAPACITY, not the floor. The evening's P14 conversation was aimed at the wrong bound.**

---

## 4. And in its honest form it is not a constitutional question at all

The capacity's open piece is **not** *"should this be a fourteenth primitive?"* It is **Target #15's already-open residual**: derive the local capacity law

$$\Gamma_0(\rho) \to 0 \ \text{ as } \ \rho \to \rho_{\max}$$

**from P01–P13.** That is a **derivation target the corpus already carries**, has **already banked one negative against**, and already describes in its own words as *"a real open problem, not a quick win."*

> **So the P14 question does not merely dissolve. It relocates.** `N_bw`'s floor is a gap under an object nothing depends on. **The load-bearing bandwidth gap is the capacity, it has been open since July under a different name, and it is a derivation problem rather than a constitutional one.**

---

## 5. Why this also resolves the tension in the two instincts

The apparent conflict was: *something foundational is missing* versus *P11 is nearly the only primitive, everything reduces downward.*

**If the missing thing is the capacity, there is no conflict.** A **derived** per-cell capacity is **P11 and P04 doing more work**, not a new primitive doing some. *That is the direction the substrate has always been read, and it is what a successful `Γ₀(ρ)` derivation would deliver.*
