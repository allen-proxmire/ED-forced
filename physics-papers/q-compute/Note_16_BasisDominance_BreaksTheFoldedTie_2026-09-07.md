# Target #16 — the folded/unfolded tie is breakable without building the map

**2026-09-07.** Target #16 is flagged as *"ED's single most load-bearing current gap"* and, in the same breath, as *"a genuine research frontier, not a session win; forcing it risks over-banking."* **Both are right, and this note does not build the map.** It removes the map's blocking role from **one** of the four threads #16 gates — the one that carries the matter-wave weapon's only falsifiable directional content.

---

## 1. What is actually blocked

`theory/MatterWave_Direction_and_StructureToChannel_Map_2026-07-24.md` states the position honestly:

> **Net: ED does not currently determine whether folded or unfolded goes classical first.** Neither direction is an ED prediction; presenting either would be over-banking.

It lists four reasons. **The first is the load-bearing one, and the other two that matter hang off it:**

> **1. The IPR premise is one of two regimes, not forced.** In the plain site basis, folding leaves the marginal `{b_K}` unchanged → `M_eff` unchanged. **Any effect requires moving to the collective-mode basis.**
> **2. Basis pinned only in *type*, not in the assignment.**
> **3. ED's own two papers point OPPOSITE ways.** `Paper_056`'s IPR framing leans *unfolded*-first; `Paper_060`'s "internal-rule-type-content" framing leans *folded*-first.

> **So the direction is not blocked on the whole structure→`{b_K}` map. It is blocked on one binary: is the einselected basis SITE-LOCAL or COLLECTIVE?** Site-local and folding changes nothing. Collective and it does.

## 2. The corpus already contains the criterion — in a different arc

`state-reduction/StateReduction_ChannelGranularity.md`, pillar 2 and the germanium case:

> *"When the self-Hamiltonian (the binding, ~eV) dominates the collapse/gravitational coupling (tiny), einselection makes the **energy eigenbasis** the pointer basis (**the standard Paz–Zurek result for a dominant self-Hamiltonian**)."*

**That is exactly the missing selector.** Paz–Zurek is a *competition*, and its outcome is set by which term dominates:

| regime | einselected basis | folding effect |
|---|---|---|
| environmental interaction dominates | **position / site-local** | none — marginal `{b_K}` unchanged |
| **self-Hamiltonian dominates** | **energy eigenbasis** — for a macromolecule, **collective normal modes** | folding correlates modes → `M_eff` falls |

**And a matter-wave interferometer sits unambiguously in the second regime, by construction.** The entire experimental design is the suppression of environmental coupling — high vacuum, cryogenic, deliberately isolated — while the self-Hamiltonian (covalent bonding, ~eV) is untouched and enormous. *An experiment that failed to be in this regime would not show interference at all, so any run that produces data is in it.*

> **The einselected basis for a matter-wave macromolecule is collective. Objection 1 is answered, and it is answered by ED's own stated criterion rather than by a new assumption.**

## 3. What that does to the tie

**Objection 1 falls** — the collective regime is the operative one, so the IPR premise is not an arbitrary choice of two.

**Objection 2 falls with it** — Paz–Zurek pins the *assignment*, not merely the type, once the dominance ratio is known, and the ratio is fixed by the apparatus.

**Objection 3 resolves toward `Paper_056`.** In the collective basis, folding's non-covalent tertiary contacts couple otherwise-independent segments, correlating the modes and lowering the effective independent channel count in `M_eff = (Σ_K b_K)²/(Σ_K b_K²)`. **Lower `M_eff` sits further from `M_cap`, so the folded molecule survives longer:**

$$\boxed{\text{unfolded goes classical first, at equal mass.}}$$

**Objection 4 is unaffected** — it was a withdrawn category error (thermodynamic conformational entropy is a mixture, not a coherent channel count) and does not bear either way.

## 4. Tier, stated before anyone quotes this

**`A → position`. This is not a prediction and must not be presented as one.**

- The Paz–Zurek criterion is **in the corpus** and is standard decoherence theory. **Solid.**
- That matter-wave interferometry sits in the self-Hamiltonian-dominant regime is an **inference from the apparatus**, short and hard to argue with, but an inference. **Grounded-in-kind.**

> **What this note establishes is that the tie is breakable at all, and by which criterion.** *It does not establish the size of the effect, and without a size there is no experiment: two arrangements that differ by 0.1% in `M_eff` are not distinguishable at any mass.*

## 5. What is still owed, and it is smaller than #16

**Not the full structure→`{b_K}` map.** Only this: **given the collective basis, compute `M_eff` for a folded and an unfolded configuration of the same molecule and report the ratio.** Normal-mode structure of a protein is standard computational chemistry; the ED-specific step is the bandwidth weighting.

**If the ratio is order-unity, the weapon has a directional prediction and it is only-ED** — no mass-based rival (QM, CSL, Diósi–Penrose) distinguishes two arrangements of identical mass. **If it is a fraction of a percent, the direction is real and untestable, and that should be said.**

**And the condition that would flip it is now nameable, which it was not before:** an experiment in which environmental coupling dominates the self-Hamiltonian would einselect the site basis, and the direction would vanish rather than reverse. **That is a stated regime boundary on an ED claim, which is the shape a falsifiable prediction is supposed to have.**
