## 2. Sakharov's move, and the two things he presupposes

Following Visser: Einstein gravity has two ingredients — (1) the pseudo-Riemannian geometry (from the equivalence principle) and (2) the field equations for the Ricci tensor. **Sakharov addresses only (2).** He assumes you are handed a Lorentzian manifold, makes no assumption about its dynamics, does *not* quantize the geometry, and quantizes everything else. Then the one-loop matter effective action is *guaranteed* to contain

$$ \int d^4x \sqrt{-g}\,\{c_0 + c_1 R(g) + c_2\,(\text{“}R^2\text{”})\}, $$

which is, term for term, the cosmological constant, the Einstein-Hilbert action `R/16\pi G`, and higher-curvature corrections. Using the heat-kernel expansion with a short-distance cutoff `κ` (dimensionally `[s] = κ^{-2}`), the divergences organize by power:

- a `κ⁴` divergence induces the cosmological constant, `Λ ~ κ⁴`;
- a `κ²` divergence induces Newton's constant, `\tfrac{1}{G} \approx -\tfrac{1}{2\pi}\,\mathrm{str}[k_1]\,κ^2` (Visser eq. 29), so **`G⁻¹` scales as the cutoff squared, weighted by the particle spectrum** through the Seeley-DeWitt coefficient `k_1`;
- logarithmic divergences induce the curvature-squared couplings.

The central lesson Sakharov draws, and the one this article builds on, is **not** the particle-spectrum formula but the deeper structural fact Visser states in his conclusion:

> *"Deriving the inverse square law, and all of Einstein gravity, is in fact automatic once you have demonstrated the existence of Lorentzian manifolds — reasonably large reasonably flat arenas on which to set up a low-energy QFT."*

In other words, Sakharov **reduces the problem of gravity's dynamics to the problem of the arena's existence.** Given a manifold, the dynamics comes for free at one loop. The two things left owed to a deeper theory are therefore precisely: **the manifold** (Sakharov assumes it) and **the cutoff** (Sakharov leaves it to Planck-scale physics). ED supplies both.

(This is a claim about the *source* of the rigidity, not about its spatial variation: like Newton's constant in either picture, ED's `M_P^2` is essentially constant, set by the grain, not tracking local matter density.)

## 7. Position statement

Sakharov's induced gravity is often read as "gravity comes from the vacuum fluctuations of matter." Visser's sharper reading is that Sakharov *reduced* gravity to the existence of an arena: given a Lorentzian manifold to host a quantum field theory, all of Einstein gravity is automatic at one loop, and the only things owed to a deeper theory are the manifold and the cutoff. Event Density is a candidate for that deeper theory, and it supplies exactly those two owed things: it **builds** the arena as the coarse-grained reading of a discrete participation substrate — an emergent Lorentzian metric with, for once, a *real medium* beneath it — and it **is** the cutoff, the Planck grain that fixes `G = c^3\ell_P^2/\hbar`. It even supplies a substrate-native gravitational rigidity of the right strength (`~κ²`), sourced by the substrate's standing connectivity rather than by a matter loop, so that ED does not wait on a particle spectrum to set `G`. What ED does not yet do is compute the induced action on its own arena, close its own curvature-emergence bridge, or derive Λ's value — though on the cosmological constant it dissolves the catastrophe (its discreteness leaves no infinite tower to overshoot) and inherits only the value via `H_0`, rather than sharing Sakharov's embarrassment. The claim is therefore precise and bounded: **induced gravity presupposes a medium and a cutoff; Event Density supplies both, and gives the medium a real substrate — the arena Sakharov assumed, now built rather than borrowed.** None of this is offered as confirmed physics; it is offered as a grounding of the inputs Sakharov's construction was waiting for.
