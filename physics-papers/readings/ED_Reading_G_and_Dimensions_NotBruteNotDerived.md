## 1. The move ED refuses, and the honesty it forces
Ask a working physicist why Newton's constant has the value it does, or why space has three dimensions and time one, and the honest modern answer is often a shrug: these are inputs, measured and plugged in, and there may be no deeper reason. Carroll put the general version of this well in his recent conversation with Greene: always keep asking whether there is a deeper level, and be prepared for the answer to be no.

Event Density refuses the shrug, but it has to earn the refusal without overreaching, and this is the one topic where overreaching is fatal. "Why 3+1 dimensions" is where theories go to embarrass themselves. So the whole value of this reading is in drawing the line precisely: ED shows that `G` and `3+1` are *not brute*, that there is real structure behind them, while being just as clear that "not brute" is not "derived from nothing." Both statements have to be made, or the piece is either a shrug or a crank manifesto.

`G = c³ ℓ_P² / ħ`, and in `d` spatial dimensions, `G_d = c³ ℓ_P^(d−1) / ħ`.

The mechanism is the holographic count. A causal boundary of radius `R` carries `N = 4πR²/ℓ_ED²` severed channels, and feeding that count into the cumulative-strain potential returns Newton's law with `G` fixed by the grain size, not chosen. `G` is "not an independent primitive; it is a downstream structural identification" (Paper_027). And the dimensional structure is telling: in `G_d = c³ ℓ_P^(d−1)/ħ`, the power of `c` is dimension-blind, and the spatial dimension lives *entirely* in the `ℓ_P^(d−1)` factor, which is the holographic area element. The constant's units know about the area law because they came from it.

Now the honest tier, three cuts of it:
- The **form** is derived; the **value** is inherited, through the single postulated identification `ℓ_ED = ℓ_P`, which the corpus itself calls "the empirically-correct choice, not a substrate-level derivation." `ℓ_P` is a units anchor, so pinning `G`'s value to it is not the same as computing a pure number.
- The famous "`G` three ways" (Jacobson's entropy density, Padmanabhan's degree-of-freedom count, the constants-ledger grain) is **one fact seen three ways** (`n = 4S`), a consistency check, not three independent derivations. The corpus says so in its own text. Anyone who counts it as three confirmations is inflating.
- So the defensible claim is narrow and real: *`G` is neither fundamental nor free; its form is fixed by the grain and the area law, and only its value is inherited through the Planck length.* That is a genuine step past "brute measured constant," and it is not "we derived `G`."

## 3. 3+1 dimensions: the crank-magnet, handled carefully
Start with the part ED does *not* have to argue for. The **"+1," time, is native.** The arrow (commitment, P11) is the one primitive ED starts from, not a dimension it selects. ED does not derive time; it begins with it and builds the rest downstream. So the "+1" is honest bedrock, not a result and not an embarrassment.

The **"3," the spatial dimensions, is where care is everything.** ED has genuine *selections* of three, and, deflated, there are two independent ones:

1. **1D–1D linking, unique to three dimensions.** ED's relata are one-dimensional chains (P01) carrying committed order (P11). Two one-dimensional objects can link and stay non-separable only in three dimensions (the codimension count `p+q = N−1` gives `1+1 = N−1`, so `N = 3`). This is the genuinely independent route, and it is arrow-routed: the thing needing a topological protector is the committed order the chains carry. A simulation check even finds the order is held geometrically rather than by a cheap relabeling (a certified result at roughly `τ ≈ 0.96`), which rules out the deflationary alternative. Tier: a structural bridge with one open premise.

2. **The holographic count.** The reach law that yields the gravitational metric `g ~ 1/b` reproduces the weak-field GR result *uniquely in three dimensions*. Important deflation: the inverse-square Gauss law and `G`'s `ℓ_P^(d−1)` dimensional factor are **not** separate roads to three, they are the same holographic cut wearing different clothes. So this is one independent selection, not three.

Two independent structures, linking and the holographic count, converge on the same number. That convergence is suggestive and it is worth showing. It is *not* a derivation, and here is the tier stated flat, because this is the sentence that matters most:

> Spatial dimension is **postulated** in ED (the P06 primitive). The linking and holographic routes are **selections and structural bridges anchored on already-observed content, gated on curvature emergence**, not a from-nothing derivation. The corpus's own current verdict, calibrated down from an earlier paper's more confident heading, is "structural bridge plus one open premise, **not** 'ED forces 3D.'"

There is a circularity ED is honest about: you arguably need a three-dimensional substrate even to *pose* the linking question, which is why these routes are gated on the still-open problem of deriving the geometry itself.

And the cleanest honesty signal of all: ED **tried to close it and failed, on the record.** The attempt to upgrade "at least three" to "exactly three" from the primitives was killed twice over: a linking number is *symmetric* and so cannot carry the *direction* of committed order (that job belongs to the arrow, P11, not to the link), and a one-dimensional chain can link a two-dimensional membrane in four dimensions just as emergently as two chains link in three, so four dimensions reopens. The corpus records this as a failed closure with a "do not re-attempt" flag. A framework that files its own failed proofs is doing the opposite of what a crank does.

One more distinction, so the account does not oversell a coincidence. There is a *second* "three" in ED, the internal dimension of the amplitude space that carries the Standard Model's gauge structure. That internal three is a different object from the three of physical space. Identifying the two, the "one knot" idea, is an account the corpus itself flags as genuinely speculative, not a result.
