# The Universe Reproduced From Thirteen Primitives
#### Why Event Density's Breadth Is Evidence, Not Bookkeeping

Allen Proxmire · 2026-07-27

## The claim
Event Density reproduces the *form and mechanism* of many separate, currently-unrelated domains of physics from thirteen substrate primitives plus a small number of labeled structural postulates.

The room in a mature science to predict what the incumbents cannot test is nearly full. What ED claims is narrower and, taken seriously, harder to dismiss: **one structure gives the shape of the whole landscape, where the standard picture needs several mutually-incompatible theories and a pile of brute inputs.**

## The objection, met head-on
The reflex is immediate and fair to state: *"So what? You fit facts that were already known. Any flexible theory can be reverse-engineered to match what we already measured."*

That reflex is a **proxy**, and it's aimed at the wrong target. It treats "known versus new" as the thing that matters. It isn't. On any careful accounting of evidence, a fact confirms a theory to the degree it would be *surprising unless the theory were true*. Whether the fact was discovered before or after the theory never enters that reasoning. The importance of *when* a fact is discovered is psychological, not logical. What the "you just fit it" reflex is *actually* reaching for is a real and legitimate question:

> **Could you have tuned your theory to fit this?**

A brand-new prediction answers it outright: the fact didn't exist before the theory was built, so the theory couldn't have been fudged or dialed in. But novelty is *one* way to guarantee no-fudging, not the only one. A known fact reproduced by a structure too rigid to have been fitted to it carries just as much weight. So the real question is not old-versus-new. It is **tunable-versus-untunable.** And that is a question you can actually answer.

## The untunability argument
Two moves, and the whole case for taking Event Density seriously rests on them.

**1. The ratio. Few knobs, many outputs.**
"This explains everything" from a theory with a thousand free parameters is worth nothing, because a thousand-knob theory fits anything. The same words from a theory with *thirteen* primitives are worth a great deal, *because you cannot tune thirteen things to independently hit gravity and the Born rule and viscoelastic relaxation and the dark sector by accident.* The figure of merit for a unified theory is:

> **domains-reproduced divided by free-inputs.**

That ratio is the argument, and the standard picture scores badly on it, because "GR + QM + a dark-matter particle + brute constants" is four theories in a trenchcoat, each with its own inputs, two of them not even compatible (they coarse-grain time in different ways).

An honest footnote, because a skeptic will reach for it: ED also carries *inherited values* (c, ħ, ℓ_P, H₀, the masses). Those are real, and they are not zero. But they are not a fresh knob per domain; they are the *handful* of scales that recur everywhere. ℓ_P sets Newton's constant *and* the area law *and* the Planck mass; H₀ sets the MOND scale *and* the cosmological constant. So the value layer is shared and constrained too, not tunable domain by domain. The rigidity holds at every level. (The Standard Model of particle physics inherits its constants the same way — nobody counts that against it.)

**2. The shared structure. Fit one, and the rest are forced.**
The reproductions do not each get their own private derivation. They run through the *same* primitives, so fixing the structure in one domain constrains what it does in all the others. The cleanest *cross-domain* specimen is the substrate's own dynamics — its selection functional, Σ = Coherence − Strain − Boundary:

> Take that one structure to its uniform limit and it becomes a two-channel oscillator whose exact limits are **the RC circuit** (participation channel off) and **the RLC circuit** (fully coupled). Carry the *same* dynamics into soft matter and it gives the **universal viscoelastic relaxation law.** Three faces — two circuits and a rheology law — out of one substrate structure. You could not have fitted it to the RC and then found the RLC and the relaxation law sitting there by luck; a structure you tuned to any one of them does not hand you the others.

Honest tier on that, kept in view: the soft-matter relaxation is measured and the circuits are textbook — what ED adds is that they are *one structure's* limits. The specific coefficients are inherited, and the one thing still owed is the four-run breadboard test that would confirm the circuit *coupling* directly (designed, ~$50, an afternoon, not yet run). Structure from the substrate; coefficients inherited; that last confirmation pending.

Untunability also lands in single numbers. The oldest: fit gravity to the everyday limit and you have set how it warps *time*; the same structure then *forces* how it warps *space*, so starlight must bend by exactly twice the naive value — the 1.75 arcseconds Eddington measured in 1919. You cannot fit the apple and then dial the starlight. And from cosmology: the MOND scale, a₀ = cH₀/2π, no free coefficient, just the speed of light times the Hubble rate over 2π, landing within about ten percent of the measured value. No knob to turn — you get that number from the structure or you don't.

## What this is NOT
- It is **not** "ED derives the numbers." Most values are inherited. What is unified is the *form and mechanism*, not the constants. Say that first, always.
- It is **not** out-predicting GR or QM in the tested regime. There is almost no room there, and that is honest.
- It is **not** unfalsifiable, which is the other half of not-being-a-crank. There is a companion document, *22 Ways to Kill Event Density (+ Eight More)*, that is nothing but **thirty numbered kill-conditions** — each a specific number, sign, or shape with a stated way to fail it: `α₂ = 0` *exactly*, *zero* intrinsic scatter in the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation, dark energy `w = −1` with no drift, a sharp architecture-independent decoherence wall, the four-run circuit test above, and twenty-five more. Enumerating your own kill-switches, with numbers attached, is the exact opposite of what a crank does.
- It is **not** proof. Consilience *persuades*; it does not *compel*. A determined skeptic can still say "you accommodate the known facts, so what," and the only thing that closes that door is a confirmed novel prediction — for which ED has one non-Planck-suppressed candidate that is not a postdiction: the sharp quantum-to-classical boundary (honestly still a shape-claim, not a number). Consilience is the body of the case; that one prediction is the intended knockout. Both, not either.

## Bottom line
The breadth of explanation from thirteen primitives is the argument, and it holds. Not because old facts secretly count as new — they always counted. Because **one rigid structure reproducing many independent domains cannot be a coincidence and cannot be reverse-engineered.** A theory you *could* have fitted to the data explains nothing by matching it. A theory too small to have been fitted, that matches anyway — across gravity, quantum mechanics, kinematics, and the dark sector — is telling you something about the world. That is the whole of the case, stated without inflation: reproduced from thirteen, tiered in the window, the fit itself the evidence.
