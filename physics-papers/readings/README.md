# ED Readings

**New reader: start with [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md)** — the landing page (shared stance, the two rails, reading order). This README is the folder doc (genre, rules, contents).

Interpretation and positioning pieces: Event Density's *reading* of a reigning idea in physics, set against the standard reading, written to be read by people already in that conversation.

**Genre.** These are not derivations and do not pretend to be. They are "as provable" as the views they engage (Many-Worlds, pilot-wave, a specific dark-matter particle): they earn their place by coherence, parsimony, and explanatory reach, not by theorem. The only thing a reading proves is *comparative* — that the standard view carries specific costs, and that reading the same formalism through ED's primitives does not carry them. This is the Everett move and the Einstein-on-Newton move: not a proof, a better reading.

**Two rails (non-negotiable, or a reading reads as crank and backfires).**
1. **Steelman + factual rigor.** Say what the target gets right, first and honestly. Be bulletproof on the mathematics. One subtle factual error and the piece, and ED with it, is dismissed. Grace, not grievance.
2. **Tiers intact.** ED's own claims wear their honest tiers even in argument. Where the corpus says "conditional on X" or "inherited," the reading says so. The comparison still wins (the standard view's holes are real); inflating ED's side just hands a hostile reader the kill shot. Every reading carries a "what ED does NOT claim" section.

**Distribution intent.** A reading attaches to a live conversation rather than broadcasting into the void. Respond to a specific talk, paper, or claim people are already arguing about; that is where the readers are. Criticism buys a *hearing*; it is the on-ramp, not the payload. The payload that forces acceptance regardless of the messenger is a confirmed novel prediction (M_crit, the benchtop participation-envelope). Readings clear the path for it.
