## 1. The move ED refuses
The standard road to quantum gravity is: take a classical field, the metric of General Relativity, and quantize it. That road is where the infinities live, and it is the road string theory and loop quantum gravity are each trying to pave. Event Density does not take it. It does not quantize a metric. It *grows* one, from a relational substrate that has no lengths in it to begin with. Geometry is not fundamental and then quantized; it is emergent, a coarse-grained read-out of how a substrate of participating chains shares bandwidth.

That is the same "spacetime is emergent" instinct a mad-dog Everettian like Carroll has, reached from the opposite floor: not a timeless unitary wavefunction, but a substrate whose primitive act is irreversible commitment. This reading asks what gravity looks like from that floor. The short answer: you reach the tested parts of GR several ways, you never reach *pure* GR, and the places you miss it are where the interesting money is.

## 3. The anomaly, read at face value
Reproduction is the credibility layer. The interest is where ED diverges *without* breaking anything already measured, and the sharpest divergence is galactic rotation.

Here ED makes exactly the move Justin Khoury makes with superfluid dark matter: take the MOND signal at face value. The galactic regularities, flat rotation curves, the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation, a single acceleration scale `a₀`, are too tight and too universal to be scatter in dark-matter halos. Read them as a real feature of gravity, not as an accident of halo-fitting.

ED's version of that reading is specific. Gravitational strain is quadratic in a participation amplitude, `Str = |Σ P|²`, and that square splits into a diagonal term, each source's own potential, which is Newton, and an off-diagonal *interference* term between sources. MOND is the off-diagonal cross-term between a local mass and the cosmic decoupling surface, the horizon. When a mass interferes with the horizon at `R_H = c/H₀`, the cross-term modulus is `√(a_N a₀)`, the deep-MOND law, and the scale falls out as `a₀ = cH₀/2π`, parameter-free, matching the measured value to about ten percent. The slogan the corpus uses: there is no missing matter in a galaxy, only a piece of gravity that was being missed.

Tier, plainly: the MOND-as-horizon-interference reading is **standing/structural**; `a₀ = cH₀/2π` is **form-derived, value-inherited via H₀**, a roughly ten-percent **postdiction**; BTFR is standing. A parameter-free postdiction of a known anomaly is a legitimate pillar (Mercury's precession was one), but it is a postdiction, the scale was already known, and the interference *sign* is still an open residual. No inflation: this explains the *galactic* regime, and nothing more.

## 4. The hole ED does not leave
"MOND from ED" on its own would be a fraud, because MOND famously fails where dark matter succeeds: the mass of galaxy clusters, the CMB acoustic peaks, the bullet cluster's offset between mass and gas. Any reader who knows the field stops reading at a MOND-only pitch. So the honest claim has to carry both halves.

ED's frame is *two culprits, one mechanism*. The single quadratic-strain structure has two regimes:
- **Galaxies (no particle): the horizon.** The off-diagonal interference term above. No dark-matter substance in a galaxy at all.
- **Clusters and the CMB (a particle): a relic.** A committed neutral relic, the neutral-channel sibling of the baryons, produced at the same inflation/saturation exit by the same admission mechanism, uncharged so it is dark, and diluting as `ρ ∝ a⁻³`, which is *derived* from commitment-irreversibility.

And here I have to be blunt about tiers, because this is exactly where an interpretation paper earns or loses trust. The galactic side is standing. **The relic side is a candidate, and far less developed.** The `a⁻³` dilution is derived and the relic is form-complete, but its mass and abundance are inherited, bounded only to a warm, roughly-keV window rather than derived, and, in the corpus's own words, *no CMB spectrum is computed from ED*. That is the sector's headline undelivered piece, and the reading says so rather than papering over it.

Why two components and not one substance? ED tested the one-substance route (make the same stuff do both jobs) and did not adopt it: the relic's own self-interaction coarse-grains to a *canonical* kinetic term, not the non-analytic deep-MOND term, because its local self-pairing carries no horizon and therefore no `a₀`. The unification is of *mechanism*, not *substance*. Two things, one structure.

This is where ED and Khoury part on ontology even as they share the method. Superfluid dark matter is one substance in two phases: real dark matter whose phonons mediate MOND, so a galaxy contains an actual superfluid-cored halo. ED is the opposite at the galactic scale, two substances, and a galaxy contains *no* dark-matter halo at all; the rotation curve is pure horizon-interference with no particle present. The clean discriminator, if you can measure a galaxy's core for dark substance: superfluid DM says it is there, ED says it is not. What ED offers Khoury's program in trade is a first-principles `a₀` where they fit one, and a mass-independent condensation criterion that relaxes their light-boson mass tension.

The structural payoff is that ED is not forced into either single-mechanism camp's failure. It does not need dark matter to explain galaxies, so it does not inherit ΛCDM's unexplained tightness of the MOND regularities. It does not need MOND to explain clusters, so it does not inherit pure-MOND's cluster and CMB failures. Two mechanisms thread the needle both one-mechanism camps fall through, at the cost, honestly stated, of a second component that is still a candidate rather than a computed fit.
