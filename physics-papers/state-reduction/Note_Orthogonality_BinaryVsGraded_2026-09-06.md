## 2. That fact is the problem

The collapse-rate derivation needs distinguishability to be something branches **acquire over time**:

> *"the arrow … individuates them **as soon as they become** commitment-distinguishable, i.e. when the accumulated relative commitment-action reaches the commitment quantum, `E_G τ ~ ℏ`."*

**But if branches are channels, they are perfectly distinguishable from the outset.** `p(K|L) = 0` is a standing structural fact from P07, not a quantity that accumulates.

> **So if branches were channels, `τ` would be zero and collapse would be instantaneous — contradicting `τ ∝ ℏ/E_G`, which is the arc's headline result.**

**The derivation therefore REQUIRES that its branches are not channels.** That is `#137`'s layer seam, reached from the opposite end: `#137` argued it from what the substrate represents; this argues it from what P07 already guarantees. **Two independent routes, same conclusion.**

## 3. And it sharpens the gap: binary versus graded

| | index | distinguishability |
|---|---|---|
| **sense 1** — commitment / channels | `𝒦` | **BINARY.** P07 → `p(K|L) = 0`, perfect, standing |
| **sense 2** — the system/environment cut | loci | **GRADED.** `R = b_int/b_bdry` is continuous |

The concept card says sense 2 is graded in as many words: *"Fully individuated (ratio → ∞) and fully non-individuated (ratio → 0) are limits; **most real systems sit somewhere on the continuum**."*

> **The collapse derivation needs a GRADED distinguishability that crosses a threshold as `τ` accumulates. Only sense 2 is graded. But the event that does the collapsing — commitment — is sense 1, and sense 1 is binary.**

**So the missing bridge is not merely "a bridge between two senses." It is a bridge from a graded quantity to a binary one** — and *that* is what a coefficient would be: the conversion factor between how far the graded quantity has run and when the binary event fires.

> **Which is why the coefficient is missing.** `#136` showed A1 cannot supply it. `#137` showed the object it would apply to does not exist at the substrate layer. **This says what kind of thing it would have to be, and why neither sense supplies it alone.**
