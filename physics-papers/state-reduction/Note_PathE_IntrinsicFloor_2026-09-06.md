## 1. What Path E actually is

`ChannelGranularity` closes the emission-exclusion risk by three pillars, then names two residuals. **Residual 1:**

> *"The explicit ED theorem is not written … 'the pointer basis is bounded below by the operational-distinguishability floor, which is `≫ ℓ_ED`' is assembled here from them, not proved as a single ED theorem with the floor quantified. **Today's `θ_ind = 1` looks like it answers the either/or**: it is a substrate-intrinsic length, environment-free, and by `#128` it says **no locus set of linear extent below 3 `ℓ_ED` is individuated at all.**

## 2. Why it does not — the bridge does not exist

The tempting argument runs: *a branch's displaced region must itself be an individuated thing for two branches to be distinguishable as separate systems; so the pointer basis cannot be finer than the minimal individuated region; so the floor is `≥ 3 ℓ_ED`, intrinsically.*

**That argument crosses the two senses this session just spent the day separating.**

| step | sense |
|---|---|
| "region S is not individuated" (`R(S) < θ_ind`) | **sense 2** — a locus-set criterion |
| "so commitment cannot individuate those branches" | **sense 1** — a channel event |

> **`INDIVIDUATION_DISAMBIGUATION.md` establishes that the only bridge the corpus has between the two senses is `P-Commitment-Advancement`, and that postulate is about whether a commitment ADVANCES THE LOCUS — it says nothing about distinguishability.**

**So the inference needs a bridge that does not exist.** Supplying it would mean a new postulate or theorem relating locus-set individuation to channel distinguishability — **which is real work, and is not something to slip in as a step.** The honest statement is: **`θ_ind` does not answer residual 1, and it is not close to answering it.**

## 3. And it would not matter if it did

`ℓ_ED = ℓ_P ≈ 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m`. So the candidate intrinsic floor is

$$3\,\ell_{\rm ED} \approx 4.8 \times 10^{-35}\ \text{m}$$

against the scale the emission escape actually needs — **an ordinary bound charge, Å to nm, `~10⁻¹⁰ m`**.

> **That is ~25 orders of magnitude. Even granted the bridge, an intrinsic floor at 3 `ℓ_ED` does none of the work the escape requires.**

**The coarse pointer basis must stay environment-set.** Which is exactly what the arc already says, and this makes that dependence **essential rather than incidental**: residual 2's honest scoping — *"the escape is not a universal guarantee for every conceivable environment, only for the ones these experiments actually sit in"* — **is not a gap to be closed by an intrinsic floor. It is the right shape of the claim.**

## 4. What this is worth

**Residual 1's either/or gets a partial answer: the two horns are not symmetric.**

- **Environment-set floor** — operative, `≫ ℓ_ED`, and what the escape rests on.
- **Intrinsic floor** — plausibly exists, unreachable from `θ_ind` without a new bridge, and **~25 orders of magnitude too small to be the operative one even if reached.**

**So the remaining constructive work is entirely on the A1 / channel-capacity side** — quantifying the *operational* distinguishability floor set by what the available channels resolve. **`θ_ind` and the locus-side machinery are not the tool for it**, and an hour spent trying to make them fit would have been an hour wasted.

**Nothing in this arc changes.** No tier moves, no claim is weakened, and the escape stands exactly as it stood. **What is recorded is that one tempting route into residual 1 is closed, with the reason and the number.**
