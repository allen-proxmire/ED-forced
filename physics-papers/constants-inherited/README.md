# The Constants Are Inherited

**What this folder is.** The single findable home for Event Density's position on the fundamental constants: **ED does not derive the dimensionless constants from nothing — it inherits them.** What it *does* supply is the **order and the kind** — which constants are bare *units* present from the first event (dimensionful, `= 1` in natural units, carrying no information), and which are the universe's *inherited personality* (dimensionless, accumulated only once there is matter and a cosmos). The individual constant-derivations already live scattered across the gravity and cosmology arcs; this folder gathers the position and the one new result.

## The position in one paragraph

A single event — one commitment of the arrow — already carries three scales: **c** (the rate of becoming), **ħ** (the grain of memory / action of one commitment), **ℓ_P** (the grain of space). All three are *units*: in natural units they equal 1, and they say nothing about *which* universe you live in. The dimensionless numbers that give our universe its character — α ≈ 1/137, the mass ratios, the galactic acceleration `a₀`, the cosmological constant `Λ` — are all *late*: they need matter (bound structure) or a grown cosmos with a horizon. **Newton's `G` is the hinge:** it falls out of the three units for free (`G = c³ℓ_P²/ħ`, Paper_027) as the *first* emergent constant, yet is the *least* fundamental — a combination, absent from the entire Standard Model, an exchange rate between two coarse-grained shadows (mass ↔ curvature). The first shadow, not the first thing. ED is honest that the irreducible dimensionless core is *inherited*, not derived; the claim is about order and kind, not a final theory that spits out 1/137.
