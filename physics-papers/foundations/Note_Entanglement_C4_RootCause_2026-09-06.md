## 1. The root cause

Every arc ledger heads its top section with a **qualified** name. Seven of eleven qualify it; four qualify it with the postulate caveat spelled out:

| arc ledger | its `Derived` section heading |
|---|---|
| entanglement | **"Derived (forced — but each conditional on a postulate in the Postulated table)"** |
| gravity | **"Derived (from primitives/structure; conditioning postulate noted)"** |
| qft | **"Derived (from primitives, conditional on the named postulate)"** |
| q-compute | **"Derived (from primitives, conditional on the 13 postulated primitives)"** |
| foundations | "Derived (genuine structural consequences on the postulates)" |

And the spreadsheet's own `Tier Key` defines:

> **Derived** — *"Forced/proven from the 13 primitives (+ standard math) **with NO paper-specific postulate** — the strongest claim tier."*

> **The arc ledgers and the spreadsheet use the same word to mean opposite things.** The ledgers say *"derived given the conditioning postulate, which we note"*; the sheet says *"derived, no postulate."* **The sheet is fed by the ledgers.** A row transcribed from section heading to `Tier` column is therefore over-promoted by construction — the qualifier lives in the parenthetical and the parenthetical does not travel.

**This is not overclaiming and it is not concealment.** Both documents are internally coherent and both are honest. **A word collided.** It explains the GR/QFT six, these four, and the shape of the whole C4 list, and it predicts exactly where else to look.

**It also explains why the sheet's `Inherited/Open` column is right on every row while the `Tier` column is wrong.** The column was copied faithfully from the ledger's cell; the tier came from the heading.

## 3. Row 51 is a different defect: the sheet is stale, not mistranscribed

`Paper_065` **and** the entanglement arc ledger both narrowed the monogamy claim on **2026-09-04**, and **split it into two rows**. The spreadsheet kept the pre-split wording, tier and `Status: current`.

What the ledger established: the derivation gives `Σᵢ ℰ_{A,Bᵢ} ≤ W_max`, a bound by a **chain-local constant**. CKW bounds by a **state-dependent** quantity. Strengthening `P-Measure-Saturation` to close the gap yields **equality**, which destroys the strict inequality — and the strict inequality *is* the physics, since the deficit is the residual three-tangle.

**The real result survives and is worth keeping:** a finite shared budget capping total pairwise entanglement, with maximal exclusivity (`ℰ_{A,B₁} = W_max ⇒ ℰ_{A,Bᵢ} = 0`) following. **That is genuinely monogamy-flavoured and it is the right kind of mechanism. It is not CKW.**

So the sheet now carries both rows, matching the ledger:
- **row 51** — the budget cap, `Grounded`, which *is* derived given the two postulates.
- **row 406** (appended, so no existing row number shifts) — **Monogamy in CKW form**, `Grounded — consistent with, not forced by`.

**One discrepancy left standing rather than resolved silently:** `Paper_065`'s audit table labels the CKW step **`A→consistency`**; the arc ledger's claim row calls it **`Grounded`**. Tiered here to the arc ledger, with the paper's weaker step-level label recorded in `Rests On`.
