# Condition (C) multi-seeded: no crystallization anywhere — but the canonical terms fail the *other* way

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** **Result.** Ten seeds on the physical condition. The July headline survives; a different problem surfaces.

## 0. What counts as success, since the last few notes reported numbers without saying it

**Crystallization is the failure mode, not the target.** The companion result is *"Knots, Not Crystals"* — the certified substrate has **no long-range ordering coupling**, so a term that produces crystalline order is Knots-violating.

**Total disorder is also failure.** `Paper_PhaseCoherence_P12Coh` exists to supply the **attractive/constructive sign** that V5's clock-binding and the MOND interference term previously assumed. A term that binds nothing supplies nothing.

**The target is the middle: finite reach — `ξ` finite and non-zero.** §5.4 states the window explicitly: too weak a connection *"crystallizes"*, too strong gives *"`ξ < 1`: complete decorrelation, **no binding at all**."*

## 1. The result

Ten seeds, 64×64, condition (C) — quenched bandwidth **and** `ρ` holonomy, the physical case:

| arm | `k_phase = 0` (control) | `k_phase = 1` | `k_phase = 8` |
|---|---|---|---|
| **no phase term** | `ξ = 2.1`, **0/10 crystal** | — | — |
| **`Grad`** | — | `ξ = 1.8`, 0/10 | `ξ = 0.8`, 0/10 |
| **old "Coh"** `\|acc\|/n` | — | **`ξ = 4.1`**, 0/10 | **`ξ = 3.8`**, 0/10 |
| **CANONICAL `Coh`** | — | `ξ = 1.3`, 0/10 | `ξ = 0.7`, 0/10 |

**Nothing crystallizes. 0/10 in every arm at every coupling, with tight ranges.**

## 2. The good news, stated first because it is real

**`Paper_PhaseCoherence_P12Coh`'s headline holds across ten seeds and all three readings.** In the physical condition the substrate does **not** crystallize — it stays Knots-safe. That was the claim the July arc rests on, it was resting on a single seed, and **it now has ten and survives all of them.**

**And it is not a near miss.** `R ≈ 0.01–0.07` throughout, against `R > 0.8` for a crystal verdict. Nothing is close to the boundary.

## 3. The problem, which is in the `ξ` column and not the verdict column

**Compare each arm against the `k_phase = 0` control, `ξ = 2.1`.** That control is the right baseline: phases are still deposited, but they do not influence which candidate wins, so it is exactly *"what happens without the phase term."*

- **old "Coh"** `|acc|/n`: **`2.1 → 4.1`.** The phase term **raises** the correlation length. It binds.
- **`Grad`**: `2.1 → 1.8 → 0.8`. **Lowers** it, monotonically in coupling.
- **CANONICAL `Coh`**: `2.1 → 1.3 → 0.7`. **Lowers** it further, and faster.

**Both canonical terms sit below the no-phase control at every coupling tested, and both fall to `ξ < 1` by `k_phase = 8` — which is the paper's own stated failure: "complete decorrelation, no binding at all."**

**So there is no coupling at which either canonical term improves phase correlation over having no phase term.** The one arm that supplies the constructive binding the paper was written to establish is **`|acc|/n`** — which ledger #104 showed is neither `Coh` nor `Grad`, but `Grad` divided by the coordination number.

## 4. What this does and does not say

**Does say:** the paper's **Knots-safety** claim is robust — ten seeds, three readings, no crystallization, not close. **And its constructive-binding claim is operationalization-dependent**: it holds for the `|acc|/n` form the probe used and fails for both canonical terms, which de-correlate relative to no phase term at all.

**Does not say the canonical terms are wrong.** `ξ` is measured on a growth morphology, and the phase bonus changes *which sites get selected*, not only how phases align. **A term that biases growth toward high-`|acc|` sites changes the front's geometry, and some of the `ξ` change is that rather than de-correlation per se.** Separating those two is not done here and is the obvious follow-up.

**Does not settle `Coh` versus `Grad`.** Both behave the same way here — down, with `Coh` down harder, consistent with #106's finding that the neighbour–neighbour term adds alignment drive.

## 5. Where this leaves the arc

**Two claims, now separated, which they were not before:**

1. **Knots-safety in the physical case** — **robust**, ten seeds, every reading. Stronger than it was.
2. **The attractive/constructive sign** — **holds only for `|acc|/n`.** Since that functional is neither canonical term, **the sign the V5 clock-binding and MOND-interference results were promised is currently supplied by an operationalization the corpus has no other reason to prefer.**

**Claim 2 is the live problem**, and it is a different problem from the one this whole line has been chasing since 2026-09-05. **It is also more consequential**, because two downstream results cite that sign.

**Held to the same bar as a positive:** claim 1 is a confirmation and is reported as one. Claim 2 is a **negative**, found by adding a control and ten seeds to a run that previously had neither.
