# §5.1 settled: `Paper_072`'s "rule-type" is a wording ambiguity, not a third index set — and one of its two readings breaks the paper's own thesis

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** **Settled.** No third sense of individuation; no structural change to the entanglement arc.
**Question:** `INDIVIDUATION_DISAMBIGUATION.md` §5.1 — `Paper_072` says P11 selects a unique *rule-type assignment*; canonical `Paper_087` §P11 collapses to a single *channel*. Same thing, or a different index?

---

## 1. The structure, which is not in dispute

- **P03** supplies the channel index set `𝒦`; **P07** gives channels intrinsic identities.
- **P10** makes rule-type *"a primitive label"* — matter / gauge / kernel.
- **Branch 3 (2026-09-05, canonical)** established that classifying `𝒦(u)` by rule-type *"reads a label the substrate already carries"*.

> **So channels carry rule-type labels, and rule-type is a COARSER partition of the same index set.** Selecting a channel determines a rule-type; selecting a rule-type does **not** determine a channel.

**P11 selects the finer thing.** Canonical: *"a chain's multi-channel participation collapses to single-channel participation."*

## 2. The evidence that this is wording, not structure

**`Paper_072` uses "rule-type" 5 times and the word "channel" ZERO times.** It is the only paper in its own chain that does:

| source | index used |
|---|---|
| `Paper_087` §P11 — cited by 072 | **channel** |
| `Paper_054` UR-1 — cited by 072 as its unresolved-regime source | **channel** (*"multi-channel → single-channel"*) |
| `Papers_063–068` — the entanglement arc's own machinery | **channel**: `Ψ_KL^AB` on `𝒦_A × 𝒦_B` |
| **`Paper_072`** | **rule-type**, exclusively |

**A paper that cites two channel-indexed sources and sits on top of channel-indexed machinery, while using neither the word nor the index, is doing vocabulary, not physics.**

## 3. The two readings, and why only one survives

**"Rule-type assignment" admits two readings, and the paper never disambiguates because it never says "channel" at all.**

**(a) The label reading** — *"assignment"* = the rule-type label carried by the selected channel. Then *"multiple rule-type assignments coexist"* means **two or more distinct rule-types are live**.

> **This reading breaks the paper's own thesis.** A bipartite entangled state is a superposition over channel *pairs* (`Papers_063/064`, Schmidt rank > 1). **Nothing requires those channels to differ in rule-type** — a two-qubit entangled state is matter rule-type throughout. **So under (a) a standard entangled state has a unique rule-type assignment and is NOT in the unresolved regime**, which is exactly what `Paper_072` exists to say it is.

**(b) The configuration reading** — *"assignment"* = the whole map of participation across channels, so *"multiple rule-type assignments coexist"* means **several distinct participation configurations are simultaneously supported**. **This is equivalent to the channel statement** and is consistent with P11, with `Paper_054`, and with the arc's machinery.

> **Settlement: reading (b) is the only one consistent with the paper's own claim, so it is what is meant. There is no third index set, and no third sense of "individuation".**

## 4. What follows

**Nothing structural changes.** `Paper_072` remains tiered `Asserted` (`A→position`, interpretive framing, does not modify QM predictions), the entanglement arc is untouched, and `INDIVIDUATION_DISAMBIGUATION.md` stays a **two**-sense document.

**What is owed is a wording fix, and it is small:** five instances of *"rule-type assignment"* → *"channel assignment"* (or *"participation configuration"*), plus one use of the word "channel" somewhere in the paper so the index is stated once. **Proposed, not applied** — it narrows the phrasing of a published paper in a nominally closed arc, which is the author's call, and the same convention this ledger used for `Paper_030` §3.2 (`#36` proposed, `#37` applied on instruction).

**One thing the check did earn beyond the answer.** `Paper_072`'s abstract says the identification *"composes: (i) UR-1 (Paper_054) … (ii) V5 (Paper_090) … (iii) P11"*. **`Paper_054`'s UR-1 and P11 are not independent inputs** — `#132` established that `Paper_054` §3.4's `Γ_individuation` **is the rate of P11**, in P11's own words. **So (i) and (iii) are one commitment counted twice in a three-part composition.** That does not make the identification wrong — it is an `A→position` either way — but **a composition of three is being claimed where two are the same primitive**, and that is worth recording where the paper claims its support.
