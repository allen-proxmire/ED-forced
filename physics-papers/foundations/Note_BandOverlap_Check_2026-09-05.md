# They overlap. Branch 3's free additivity fails, and what has to be postulated is a system/environment cut ED does not have

*Foundations working note, 2026-09-05 (late). Tests the one load-bearing assumption introduced by `Note_FourBand_Branch3_2026-09-05.md`. **It does not hold, and this note weakens my own recommendation from an hour earlier.***

---

## Verdict

**Adjacency and Environmental overlap by mechanism, and the corpus says so in three steps:**

1. **Decoherence in ED *is* V5-mediated.** `substrate-evaluation/Paper_QuantumDarwinism_RecordBandwidth` §3, table row: *"**Imprinting (decoherence)** | **V5-mediated cross-chain correlation formation**, retarded, finite-memory `τ_V5` | `Paper_090` §3."*
2. **V5 requires P05.** `Paper_090` closing statement: *"V5's identity is **conditional** on the postulated primitives P02 + P04 + **P05** + P07 + P09 + P10 + P11 + V1 inheritance."*
3. **Therefore every Environmental (decohering) channel is a P05-transporting channel.**

**So under the card's own wording — Adjacency = *"content shared with neighbouring loci **via P05 polarity-transport**"* — the Environmental class is not disjoint from Adjacency. It is arguably contained in it.**

**Consequence: P04's additivity does not give the band totals.** The sums double-count. **Branch 3's central free ride — "the classification is disjoint, so additivity delivers the bands" — does not hold as I stated it an hour ago.**

---

## 1. The repair that exists, and what it costs

The two bands can still be separated, **but by *counterparty* rather than by mechanism**:

- **Adjacency** — content shared with **neighbouring loci in the chain's own structure**.
- **Environmental** — content coupled to **the bath**.

**Both use P05. They differ in what is on the other end.** That is a coherent distinction and it is almost certainly what the card intends.

**Its cost is exact: it requires "the environment" to be a substrate-level distinguishable thing, and nothing in the primitives defines one.**

- **Canonical P11's operational content does use the word** — *"discrete, **environmentally**-phase-randomizing, irreversible substrate events"* — so the notion is in the primitives. **But it is used, not defined.** P11's statement proper says only *"the un-selected channels' phase content randomized."*
- **No primitive supplies a system/environment cut.** P01–P13 give loci, channels, chains, rule-types and commitment; **none of them partitions "my structure" from "the bath."**

## 2. So the postulate is not what I said it was

`Note_FourBand_Branch3` §3 said the residue was *"the classes are disjoint and exhaustive over `𝒦(u)`, and `b(u)` is bounded."*

**That understated it.** Disjointness is not an independent assumption you can simply name — **it presupposes a substrate-level system/environment cut, and supplying that is a substantially harder commitment than declaring a partition.** It is, in its usual form, the factorization / Heisenberg-cut problem.

**Revised residue, stated honestly:**

> **(a)** a substrate-level criterion distinguishing *neighbouring locus in the chain's own structure* from *environment*; **(b)** given (a), disjointness and exhaustiveness of the four classes; **(c)** a per-locus bound on `b(u)`, which canonical P04 does not supply.

**(a) is the new one and it is the expensive one.**

## 3. This is the third arrival at the same question tonight

- **The `Str(C)` bridge** ended on *whose channels does the radial sum run over — the chain's own, or ambient structure?*
- **The layer seam** turns on what crosses when a layer-1 functional is read from layer 2.
- **And now the band classification** turns on distinguishing a chain's own neighbours from its environment.

**All three are the same question: where does the chain end and the rest begin?** Three arcs, reached independently, in one night. **That is a stronger signal than any of the three individually**, and nothing in the corpus treats it as one object.

## 4. What survives, and what this does to the arcs

**Adjacency and Commitment-reserve remain cleanly licensed.** P05 gives one; P11 gives the other. **Internal is the remainder.** Those three are disjoint by construction: transporting, reserved-for-commitment, and neither.

**A three-class reading is therefore disjoint and additive with no new postulate.** The question is whether anything needs the fourth.

**It does.** `primitives/concepts/participation_bandwidth.md` uses the Environmental band substantively — double-slit fringe visibility, qubit `T2`, *"coherence engineering in quantum hardware is ED-bandwidth engineering"*. **So Environmental is load-bearing for the QM and q-compute arcs, and dropping it is not free.**

- **Soft-matter transport survives either way.** It needs Adjacency as the sole inter-locus conduit, and Adjacency is clean under both readings.
- **The QM / q-compute usage needs Environmental as distinct from Adjacency**, and that is exactly the distinction §1 shows is undefined.

## 5. Honest status of Branch 3

**Not dead, and better than Branches 1 and 2 for the reason it was proposed** — it needs no amendment to `Paper_087` and no appeal to the archived paper. **But its headline claim was wrong.** The bands are not *derived* with additivity free; they are **classified, with disjointness resting on an undefined system/environment cut.**

**Recommendation, revised: adopt the three-class version now, and treat Environmental as an open item rather than a band.** That gives soft-matter its re-grounding immediately, keeps `Paper_087` untouched, and puts the QM/q-compute usage of "environmental bandwidth" where it belongs — **as content that awaits a substrate-level system/environment criterion, alongside the two other arcs waiting on the same thing.**
